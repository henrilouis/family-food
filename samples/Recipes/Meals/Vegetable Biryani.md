# Vegetable Biryani

## Ingredients

- *60 ml* whole milk
- *20 strands* saffron
- *400 g* basmati rice
- *2 large* yellow or white onions, halved and thinly sliced
- *70 ml* ghee or grapeseed oil
- *2½ tsp* kosher salt, divided, plus more to taste
- *125 g* dried apricots, chopped
- *70 g* whole cashews
- *35 g* golden raisins
- *10 cloves* garlic, grated
- *50 g* fresh ginger, peeled and grated
- *1* green chile, minced
- *1½ tsp* garam masala
- *1 whole* star anise
- *1 tsp* ground caraway
- *½ tsp* ground turmeric
- *120 g* plain unsweetened yogurt
- *1 small* cauliflower, cut into florets
- *1* Yukon Gold potato, peeled and diced
- *1 medium* carrot, peeled and diced
- *240 ml* water
- *60 g* green peas, fresh or frozen
- *250 ml* fresh cilantro, stems and leaves
- *250 ml* fresh mint, stems and leaves
- *60 ml* lemon or lime juice
- *3* green cardamom pods, lightly cracked
- *1 stick (5 cm)* cinnamon
- *4* cloves
- *2 large* bay leaves
- *10 ml* rosewater
- *10 ml* pandan (kewra) water

## Method

1. Warm *60 ml* milk until steaming. Grind half of *20 strands* saffron, add all the saffron to the milk, cover and steep.
2. Rinse *400 g* basmati rice until the water runs clear. Soak it in cold water for 30–60 minutes, then drain well.
3. Heat *60 ml* ghee in a Dutch oven. Fry *2 large* onions with a pinch of salt until deep golden, 15–20 minutes. Reserve half; cook *125 g* dried apricots, *70 g* cashews and *35 g* raisins with the remaining onions for 2 minutes, then reserve as garnish.
4. Return the first reserved onions to the pot. Add *10 cloves* garlic, *50 g* ginger, *1* green chile, *1½ tsp* garam masala, *1* star anise, *1 tsp* caraway and *½ tsp* turmeric; cook until fragrant. Add *120 g* yogurt, *1 small* cauliflower, *1* potato, *1 medium* carrot, *240 ml* water and *1 tsp* salt. Simmer covered until the vegetables are tender, about 20 minutes; add *60 g* peas and cook 5 minutes. Fold in *250 ml* cilantro and *250 ml* mint and transfer the vegetables to a bowl.
5. Boil the rice with *960 ml* water, *60 ml* lemon juice, *1½ tsp* salt, *3* cardamom pods, *1 stick* cinnamon, *4* cloves, *2 large* bay leaves and *5 ml* ghee for 2 minutes. Drain; it should be only partly cooked.
6. Grease the Dutch oven with the remaining *5 ml* ghee. Layer in half the rice, the vegetables and their liquid, then the rest of the rice. Sprinkle with the saffron milk, *10 ml* rosewater, *10 ml* pandan water and the reserved onion-fruit-cashew garnish.
7. Seal tightly with foil and a lid. Cook over medium-low heat for 15 minutes, then rest 5 minutes. If liquid remains at the bottom, reseal and cook 5–10 minutes longer. Fluff gently with a fork and serve.
