# Dahl

## Ingredients

- *300g* red split lentils
- *1 tbsp* sunflower oil
- *1 large* onion, finely chopped
- *2 tsp* garam masala
- *2 tsp* curry powder
- *1 tsp* paprika powder
- *1 tsp* cumin powder
- *1 tsp* cardamom
- *1* cinnamon stick
- *4cm* fresh ginger, grated
- *2* garlic cloves, pressed
- *1* red pepper, deseeded and finely chopped
- *1* green pepper, deseeded and finely chopped
- *1* winter carrot, peeled and diced small
- *2* tomatoes, diced
- *500ml* vegetable stock
- *250ml* coconut milk
- *400g* fresh leaf spinach, washed
- salt and pepper

### To serve

- *4 tbsp* sunflower oil
- *10* lime leaves
- *1 tsp* mustard seeds
- *1* naan
- *4 tbsp* semi-skimmed yoghurt
- parsley or coriander leaves

## Method

1. Rinse *300g* red split lentils under running water and drain well. Heat *1 tbsp* sunflower oil in a pan over medium-high heat and fry *1 large* onion for 3–4 minutes. Add *2 tsp* garam masala, *2 tsp* curry powder, *1 tsp* paprika, *1 tsp* cumin, *1 tsp* cardamom, *1* cinnamon stick, *4cm* ginger, *2* garlic cloves, *1* red pepper, *1* green pepper, *1* carrot and *2* tomatoes and fry for 1 minute. Add the lentils and deglaze with *500ml* vegetable stock. Bring to a boil and simmer on low heat for 15–20 minutes until the lentils are cooked.
2. Heat *4 tbsp* sunflower oil in a small frying pan and fry *10* lime leaves and *1 tsp* mustard seeds for 1 minute. Remove from heat and set aside. Warm *1* naan according to package instructions.
3. Add *250ml* coconut milk to the lentils and heat briefly. Stir in *400g* spinach in batches and let it wilt. Season with salt and pepper. Garnish the dahl with *4 tbsp* yoghurt, fresh herbs and the spiced oil with mustard seeds and lime leaves. Serve with the naan.

Variations: you can vary endlessly by using different vegetables or spices. Replace the spinach with roughly chopped kale or cavolo nero for more *bite*. Also try brown lentils or yellow split peas instead of dried red split lentils for a different flavour.
