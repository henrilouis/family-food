# Naan

*Serves 8*

## Ingredients

- *1 tsp* sugar
- *120ml* warm water
- *7g* active dry yeast
- *270g* all-purpose flour
- *125g* plain yoghurt
- *1/2 tsp* salt
- *1 tbsp* oil
- Some oil, for greasing the skillet
- *3 tbsp* melted butter, to brush with

## Method

1. In a small bowl, stir *1 tsp* sugar, *120ml* warm water and *7g* yeast together. The yeast should be activated when it becomes foamy, about 10 minutes. Put *270g* flour on a flat surface and make a well in the middle. Add the yeast mixture, *125g* yoghurt, *1/2 tsp* salt and *1 tbsp* oil, and knead the dough until the surface becomes smooth and shiny, about 10 minutes. Cover the dough and let it rise in a warm place (for example beside the stovetop or a warm oven) until doubled in size, about 1 hour.
2. Divide the dough into 8 equal portions. Roll each into an 8" circle with a rolling pin.
3. Heat a skillet (cast-iron preferred) over high heat and lightly grease it with oil so the dough doesn't stick. Place a dough circle on the skillet. When it puffs up and bubbles and burnt spots appear, flip it over and cook the other side. Repeat until all the dough is done.
4. Brush the naan with *3 tbsp* melted butter and serve warm.
